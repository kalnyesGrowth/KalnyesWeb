About J&Y

14 years transforming Fredericksburg homes

J&Y Painting & Remodeling is a locally owned home improvement company that's been serving Fredericksburg, Stafford, and surrounding areas for 14 years. We started as a painting crew and grew into a full-service remodeling company because our customers kept asking for more.

We believe a beautiful home — inside and out — makes a real difference in how you feel every day. That's why we care about every detail: proper surface prep, clean edges, level tile, smooth drywall. The difference between good and great work is in those details, and we don't cut corners on them.

Free estimates, fair pricing, and work done right — that's been our standard for 14 years and it's not changing.
