Our Story

From One Mower to 350+ Properties

Green Valley Landscaping was started in 2017 by Miguel Santos with a used truck, a zero-turn mower, and five residential clients in the Aquia Harbour neighborhood. Miguel grew up working on his family's farm in El Salvador and brought that same work ethic to Stafford County.

Today the company runs 4 full crews, 2 dedicated install teams, and serves over 350 weekly maintenance clients across Stafford, Fredericksburg, and Spotsylvania. Every crew lead has at least 3 years of experience with the company.

Miguel still does every design consultation personally. He walks the property, listens to what you want, and creates a plan that works for your budget, your soil, and Virginia's growing seasons.
