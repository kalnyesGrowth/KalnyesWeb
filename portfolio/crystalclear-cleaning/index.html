About Us

Started by a Mom Who Understood the Struggle

Crystal Clear Cleaning was founded in 2019 by Maria Gonzalez, a mother of three who spent years juggling a full-time nursing career with the impossible task of keeping a house clean with small children. She understood firsthand what busy families needed: reliable, trustworthy cleaning that actually got done right.

Maria started with three clients and her own supplies. Today the company employs 12 cleaners organized into consistent two-person teams, serving over 200 homes and 15 commercial clients across Stafford, Fredericksburg, and Spotsylvania.

Every team member goes through Maria's personal training program and a 30-day probation period before handling a client's home independently. She interviews every hire herself because she knows that trust is the product, not just cleaning.
