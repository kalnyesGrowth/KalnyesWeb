About Us

Keeping Families Comfortable Since 2013

Blue Ridge Climate Control was founded by Greg and Amanda Torres after Greg spent 12 years working for a large HVAC corporation. He got tired of seeing homeowners sold equipment they did not need, by technicians paid on commission.

So he built a company around a simple idea: diagnose honestly, price fairly, and fix it right. No commission-based upselling. No $89 diagnostic fees that become $89 "trip charges" when you hire someone else.

Today the team includes 7 EPA-certified technicians, a dedicated install crew, and Amanda running customer experience. They are a Carrier Certified Dealer and serve over 800 Comfort Club members across Stafford, Fredericksburg, and Spotsylvania.
