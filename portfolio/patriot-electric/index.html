Our Story

Built on Safety, Driven by Craft

Patriot Electric VA was started in 2009 by Marcus Boyd, a U.S. Army veteran who spent 8 years as a combat engineer before earning his master electrician license. Marcus believes electrical work should be done with the same discipline and precision he learned in the military.

What started as a solo operation out of Fredericksburg has grown into a 6-person team serving Stafford, Spotsylvania, King George, and Caroline counties. Every technician on the team is a licensed journeyman or master electrician. No apprentice-only crews, ever.

Marcus still personally inspects every panel upgrade and rewiring job before signing off. His name is on the license and on the truck, and he takes that seriously.
