What We Do

Complete Tree Care Services

From precision trimming to full-scale emergency removal — every job executed with the same standards we held in uniform.

🌲

Tree Removal

Safe, efficient removal of trees in tight spaces, near structures, or in difficult terrain. We plan the drop, protect your property, and haul everything off clean.

✂️

Tree Trimming & Pruning

Proper pruning extends tree life, improves structure, and eliminates branches that threaten your home, power lines, or vehicles. ISA-certified technique on every cut.

🌿

Crown Reduction & Shaping

Reduce wind load on large trees, clear sightlines, and improve your property's curb appeal — without harming the tree's long-term health.

🪨

Stump Grinding

Complete stump removal to below grade — no more trip hazards, no more sprouting, no more eyesore. Grindings can be left as mulch or hauled off.

⛈️

Storm Damage & Emergency

A tree on your roof or blocking your driveway needs to come down now. We respond to storm emergencies 24/7 in Stafford, Fredericksburg, and surrounding areas.

🔬

Tree Health Assessment

ISA-certified diagnosis of disease, structural weakness, pest damage, and root issues. Know if your trees are a hazard before they become an emergency.
