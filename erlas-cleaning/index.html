FAQ

Common Questions

What most customers ask before their first booking.

How much does cleaning cost?
Pricing depends on your home's size and what type of clean you need. Call or email for a free quote — we give you a real number the same day, no obligation.
Do I need to be home during the cleaning?
You don't have to be. Many clients give us a key or door code and come home to a clean house. All team members are background-checked and fully insured.
What products do you use?
We use professional-grade cleaning products. Eco-friendly and non-toxic alternatives are available on request — just let us know when you book, especially if you have kids, pets, or sensitivities.
What if something isn't cleaned to my standard?
Just call us within 24 hours and we'll come back to re-clean the area at no charge. No arguments, no hassle. Your satisfaction is the entire point.
How often should I schedule cleaning?
It depends on your household. Most families with kids or pets go bi-weekly. Couples or singles often do monthly. We'll help you figure out what makes sense for your home.
Do you bring your own equipment and supplies?
Yes — we bring everything. You don't need to supply any cleaning products, mops, vacuums, or materials. Just let us know your preferences and we take care of the rest.
