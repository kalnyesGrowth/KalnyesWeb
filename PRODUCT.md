# KalnyesGrowth — Web Design Agency

## Product Purpose
A landing page that converts local Virginia home service contractors into booked Calendly calls. The offer: Jose builds a complete custom website for the business owner first, shows it to them free, and they pay $400 only if they love it. The site must create enough trust that a skeptical local business owner who has never met Jose schedules a 15-minute call.

## Register
brand

## Users
**Primary:** Local Virginia home service contractors — electricians, plumbers, painters, roofers, HVAC techs, landscapers, handymen, tree services, pressure washers, flooring companies — in Stafford, Fredericksburg, Spotsylvania, Woodbridge, and surrounding areas. Age 35–60. Often bilingual Spanish-English. Run their own small business (2–10 employees). They distrust salespeople and don't like being tricked. They respond to directness, credibility, and seeing real proof before committing.

**Secondary:** Any local service business owner in Virginia or nearby states who finds the site through Google search.

## Offer
- Build your complete website first — you see it before paying a dollar
- $400 one-time to launch (hosting included for 1 year)
- Free if you don't love it — walk away, no charge
- 69+ demo sites already built for real Virginia businesses

## Brand Voice
Direct, confident, no-hype. Talks like a neighbor, not a salesman. In English or Spanish. Says "I built your site" not "we provide solutions." Shows the work, doesn't describe it.

## Anti-References
- Generic SaaS landing pages (floating 3D blobs, "revolutionize your workflow")
- Corporate web agencies (stock photos of people on laptops, "your partner in digital transformation")
- Cluttered multi-column feature grids
- Anything that feels like it was made in a template builder

## Strategic Principles
1. Lead with the result: "See your site — free" before explaining how it works
2. Proof before promise: show real demos first, explain the offer second
3. Remove all friction to booking: Calendly embed must be immediately accessible
4. Trust through specificity: named businesses (Tactical Arborist, Marines Service Co.), real review counts (940 reviews, 750+ reviews), real locations (Stafford VA, Fredericksburg VA)
5. Bilingual credibility: Spanish option signals "I understand your community"
6. Urgency is real: Calendly availability window, limited capacity for hand-built demos

## Current State
The page has 69+ demo cards, a Calendly embed, a hero section, and mobile sticky bar. Conversion is low because:
- Hero doesn't lead with the outcome (seeing your site)
- Too much copy before the first CTA
- Calendly embed is buried too far down
- No visible urgency
- Trust signals exist but aren't emphasized enough
