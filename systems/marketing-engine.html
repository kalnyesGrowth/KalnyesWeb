Marketing Engine

Automated email and SMS campaigns that keep your business top of mind. Seasonal promotions, customer reactivation, and drip sequences. AI writes the copy, picks the send time, and tracks the results.

How It Works

Two Automation Tracks, One System

The Marketing Engine runs two parallel workflows: proactive campaigns to your audience and automated win-back sequences for inactive customers.

Track A: Campaign Automation

1 Set Campaign Parameters

Choose your audience segment, message type (promotion, reminder, update), and delivery schedule.

2 AI Writes the Copy

Personalized messaging for each segment. The AI adapts tone, offers, and content based on service history.

3 Smart Timing

AI picks the optimal send time for each recipient based on past open and response patterns.

4 Multi-Channel Delivery

Messages go out via email and SMS simultaneously, reaching customers on whichever channel they prefer.

5 Performance Tracking

Track opens, clicks, bookings, and revenue generated. See exactly which campaigns drive results.

Track B: Customer Reactivation

1 Detect Inactive Customers

The system automatically identifies customers who have not booked in 30, 60, or 90+ days.

2 Personalize the Win-Back Message

AI crafts a message based on their past service history, referencing the exact work you did for them.

3 Deliver an Incentive

A time-limited offer specific to their service type. "15% off your next carpet cleaning this week."

4 Customer Re-engages

They book an appointment or reply to the message. AI handles the conversation and confirms the booking.

5 Feeds Back into CRM

The reactivated customer moves back into your active pipeline. Revenue recovered is tracked in your dashboard.
