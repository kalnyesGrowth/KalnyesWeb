How It Works

The System Behind Every Call

From the moment a customer dials your number to the appointment on your calendar. Every step is automated.

Step 01

Customer Calls Your Number

A customer dials your business phone number. Day or night, weekday or weekend. The call routes to your AI receptionist instantly.

Step 02

AI Picks Up in 2 Rings

Your AI receptionist answers instantly with a natural, human-like voice. No hold music. No voicemail. No "press 1 for this, press 2 for that."

Step 03

Identifies the Caller's Needs

AI asks targeted questions to understand what the customer needs. Emergency repair? Routine service? Price inquiry? It adapts the conversation based on their answers.

Step 04

Answers Questions About Your Business

Trained on your services, pricing, hours, and service areas. The AI answers accurately without putting anyone on hold. It knows your business as well as you do.

Step 05

Qualifies the Lead

Captures contact info, project details, and urgency. Filters serious buyers from tire-kickers so you spend your time on the jobs that matter.

Step 06

Books the Appointment

Checks your real-time calendar availability and books the customer into an open slot. Sends them an instant confirmation via text message.

Step 07

Notifies You Instantly

You get a notification with the caller's name, needs, and appointment details. Full transcript and recording available in your Command Center dashboard.
