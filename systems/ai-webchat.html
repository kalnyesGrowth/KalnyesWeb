How It Works

The System Behind Your Webchat

From first visit to confirmed appointment. Here is every step the AI handles for you.

Step 01

Visitor Lands on Your Website

Someone finds your site through Google, social media, or a referral. The chat widget appears in the bottom corner, ready to engage.

Step 02

AI Greets the Visitor

A friendly message opens the conversation. "Hi! Need help with [your service]? I can answer questions or get you a free estimate." No forms. No friction. Just a conversation.

Step 03

Answers Questions in Real Time

Trained on your services, pricing, and availability. The AI responds instantly to any question about your business. No waiting, no "we'll get back to you."

Step 04

Captures Lead Information

Collects name, phone number, email, and project details naturally through the conversation. No cold forms. The visitor shares their info because the AI earned their trust first.

Step 05

Books the Appointment

Checks your calendar and books the visitor into an open slot. Sends a confirmation to both of you. The visitor goes from browsing to booked in under 2 minutes.

Step 06

Lead Enters Your Command Center

Every conversation, contact, and booking flows into your dashboard automatically. You see the full history, the lead's details, and the appointment on your calendar. Nothing falls through the cracks.
