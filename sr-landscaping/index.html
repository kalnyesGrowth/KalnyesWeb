About S&R Landscaping

Fredericksburg's trusted yard crew — year after year

S&R Landscaping is a locally owned and operated property care company serving Fredericksburg, Stafford, and Spotsylvania. Owner Russell Weidner built this business on a simple standard: show up on time, do the job right, and leave your property better than you found it.
