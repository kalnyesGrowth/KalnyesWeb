About Floors & More

Fredericksburg's floor crew — trusted by neighbors

Floors & More is a locally owned flooring and tile company serving Fredericksburg and the surrounding area. Owner David Servick has been doing this work for years and built a reputation in the community for clean installs, honest pricing, and doing the job right the first time.
