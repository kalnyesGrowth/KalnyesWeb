About Us

25+ Years of Building Right

Old Dominion Deck & Fence Co. has been serving Caroline County and the surrounding Virginia area for over 25 years. We're a small, hands-on operation — which means you deal directly with the owner, not a salesperson.

We specialize in decks, fences, and waterfront docks. Every job is approached the same way: use quality materials, build it properly the first time, and make sure the customer is happy when we leave.

Based in Ruther Glen, we serve Caroline County, Fredericksburg, Stafford, Spotsylvania, and the surrounding areas. Call for a free estimate on your next outdoor project.
