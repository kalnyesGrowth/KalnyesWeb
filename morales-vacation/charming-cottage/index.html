About this place

This charming country cottage sits on five private wooded acres in Sumerduck, Virginia. A stone walkway leads to the black-paneled front door, and the wrap-around composite deck stretches across the back with Edison string lights overhead, outdoor dining, and a covered hot tub station.

Inside, open-plan living connects the marble-countertop kitchen to a farmhouse dining table and sun-filled sitting areas. Four bedrooms include a master suite with chevron wood headboard and French doors to the deck. Outside, a fire pit with Adirondack chairs, pergola swings, a giant chess set, an axe-throwing target, and a natural creek through the woods round out five acres of adventure.
