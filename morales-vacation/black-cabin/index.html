Black Luxury Cabin

Lusby, Maryland

The Approach

Nestled on a wooded hillside, the black-clad cabin rises three stories against golden hour light.

Gourmet Kitchen

Quartz countertops meet hand-hewn log walls. Full stainless suite with farmhouse sink beneath exposed timber beams.

Open Living

The kitchen flows into a sun-filled dining and living space. Industrial bar stools, rope-hung pendants, and a wood-slat island anchor the social heart of the cabin.

The Grand Staircase

Soaring double-height ceilings frame a floating staircase with cable railings. The ring chandelier hangs at loft level as LED treads light the ascent.

Upper Retreat

The master suite sits beneath the A-frame roofline with a navy accent wall and wood slat paneling. Warm log walls wrap every sleeping quarter in mountain comfort.

The Descent

Sunshine-yellow bunk quarters give way to the enclosed stairway leading down. Each step reveals what waits below.

Entertainment Level

A blue-felt pool table commands the industrial basement. Pac-Man and Simpsons arcades flank the room while a sectional sofa and coffee bar complete the hangout.

Spa Sanctuary

Floor-to-ceiling marble walls, a rain showerhead, LED-backlit mirror, and a wood-grain vanity transform the lower level washroom into a private retreat.
