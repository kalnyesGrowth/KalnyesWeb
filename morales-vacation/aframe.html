About this place

This hand-renovated A-frame cabin sits on a wooded lot in Stafford, Virginia. The soaring glass facade fills every room with natural light, and the wraparound deck makes the forest feel like an extension of the living space. Upstairs, the loft bedroom tucks under the peak with views into the canopy. Downstairs, three additional bedrooms and a full kitchen make it comfortable for families and groups.

Outside, a fire pit area with hanging egg swings and string lights turns evenings into something worth remembering. Hot tub, free parking, and fast WiFi included.
