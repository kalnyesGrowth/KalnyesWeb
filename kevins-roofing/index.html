Kevin's Roofing has been the Fredericksburg area's go-to roofing contractor since 1982. That's four decades of roof replacements, storm repairs, and satisfied homeowners who keep calling us back.

As a CertainTeed certified installer, we can offer extended manufacturer warranties that most roofers can't — because CertainTeed only certifies contractors who meet rigorous installation standards. That certification protects you long after the crew leaves your driveway.

Every roof we install starts with a free inspection. No pressure. No gimmicks. Just an honest assessment of what your roof needs — and what it doesn't.
