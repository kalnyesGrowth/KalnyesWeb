Roofing Company Website Design: Get More Leads Online

KalnyesGrowthJune 10, 20267 min read

Roofing is one of the most competitive local service industries online. Homeowners searching for roofing services are making a major financial decision, often $5,000 to $20,000 or more. Your website needs to build enough trust and credibility to win that business against dozens of competitors in your area.

What Roofing Customers Look For

Before designing your website, understand what drives a homeowner's decision when choosing a roofer:

  • Proof of quality work: Before-and-after photos, project galleries, detailed case studies
  • Trustworthiness: Reviews, licensing, insurance, manufacturer certifications
  • Clear pricing guidance: Not exact quotes, but enough information to know they're in the right ballpark
  • Responsiveness: The ability to get a response quickly, especially for storm damage or leaks
  • Local reputation: Evidence that you've worked in their neighborhood and community

Essential Pages for Roofing Websites

Service Pages

Create individual pages for each roofing service you offer:

  • Roof Replacement
  • Roof Repair
  • Storm Damage Repair
  • New Construction Roofing
  • Commercial Roofing
  • Gutter Installation and Repair
  • Roof Inspections
  • Emergency Roof Repair

Each page should target a specific keyword like "roof replacement in [city]" with unique content, relevant photos, and a clear call to action.

Location Pages

Build pages for every city, county, and major neighborhood in your service area. "Roofing contractor in Stafford, VA" is a different search from "roofer in Fredericksburg, VA," and each needs its own optimized page.

Project Gallery

A strong project gallery is your most powerful sales tool. Include before-and-after photos, the type of roofing material used, the location (city, not exact address), and a brief description of the work performed. Organize by project type so visitors can find examples relevant to their needs.

Financing Page

Roof replacements are expensive. If you offer financing, create a dedicated page explaining your options, monthly payment estimates, and the application process. This removes one of the biggest objections homeowners have and can significantly increase your conversion rate.

Trust Signals That Win Roofing Customers

Roofing is an industry where trust matters enormously. Homeowners are inviting you onto their biggest investment. Display these trust signals prominently:

  • Google reviews: Show your rating and review count on the homepage. Link to your full Google profile.
  • Manufacturer certifications: GAF, CertainTeed, Owens Corning certifications show you've met quality standards
  • License and insurance: Display your license number and mention that you carry full liability insurance and workers' comp
  • BBB rating: If you have a good BBB rating, display the badge
  • Warranties: Clearly explain your workmanship warranty and any manufacturer warranties that come with the materials
  • Years in business: Experience matters to homeowners choosing a roofer

Storm Damage and Emergency Response

Many roofing leads come from storm damage. Your website should have a dedicated section for storm damage services with:

  • A prominent "Storm Damage? Call Now" button
  • Information about the insurance claim process
  • Steps homeowners should take after storm damage
  • Your response time for emergency inspections

After major storms, search volume for "roof repair near me" and "storm damage roofer" spikes dramatically. Having optimized content for these terms means you capture those leads when demand is highest.

Design Elements That Convert

Above the Fold

Your homepage hero should include a strong headline (e.g., "Trusted Roofing in [City] for Over [X] Years"), your phone number, a "Get Free Estimate" button, your Google star rating, and a compelling image of a completed roof or your crew at work.

Free Roof Inspection or Estimate CTA

Your primary call to action should be a free roof inspection or free estimate. This is the standard offer in the roofing industry, and homeowners expect it. Make the request form simple: name, phone, address, type of service needed.

Mobile Optimization

Many homeowners search for roofers on mobile, especially during emergencies. Your site must load fast, display correctly on all screen sizes, and have a sticky click-to-call button that's always accessible.

Content Strategy for Roofing Websites

Blog content that targets homeowner questions brings in organic traffic and positions you as the local roofing expert:

  • How much does a roof replacement cost in [your state]?
  • Signs your roof needs replacement
  • Architectural shingles vs 3-tab: which is better?
  • How long does a roof replacement take?
  • What to do after storm damage to your roof
  • How to choose a roofing contractor

Each article brings in visitors who are actively thinking about roofing. They may not be ready to hire today, but when they are, your company is already their trusted source of information.

Avoid Common Roofing Website Mistakes

  • Using only stock photos instead of real project photos
  • No clear phone number visible on every page
  • Missing service area pages for cities you actually serve
  • No mention of licensing, insurance, or certifications
  • Generic "Contact Us" instead of specific "Get Your Free Estimate"
  • Slow loading speed from uncompressed gallery images
