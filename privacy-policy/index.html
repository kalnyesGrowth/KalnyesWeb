Last updated: July 10, 2026

Contact: kalnyesgrowth@gmail.com | (540) 783-8835

What Data We Collect

We collect data through the following channels:

Social Media Interactions

  • Public profile information: Your name and username as displayed on your social media profile
  • Comments: Comments you leave on our posts
  • Messages: Direct messages you send to or receive from our business accounts
  • Engagement data: Likes, shares, and other interactions with our content

Website and Service Interactions

  • Contact information: Name, email address, phone number, and business details you provide through forms, calls, or messages
  • Website analytics: Page views, session duration, and referral sources collected via our analytics tools on client websites
  • Phone and text interactions: Call recordings, transcripts, and SMS/MMS messages processed through our AI agent systems
  • Payment information: Billing details processed securely through Stripe (we do not store full card numbers)

We do not collect private information from your social media profile beyond what you make publicly visible.

How We Use Your Data

  • Responding to inquiries: When you comment on our posts, send us a message, or call our phone lines, we use your information to provide a personalized response about our services
  • Lead management: If you express interest in our services (e.g., commenting a keyword like "AUDIT" or "DEMO," filling out a contact form, or responding to an outreach email), we log your inquiry to ensure timely follow-up
  • AI-powered communications: Our AI agents may respond to your calls, texts, or chat messages using your business name and the information you provide
  • Service delivery: If you become a client, we use your contact and business information to build your website, configure your AI agents, and deliver ongoing services
  • Payment processing: We use Stripe to securely process subscription payments and one-time charges

We never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

SMS/MMS Messaging

If you provide your phone number or interact with our AI systems, you may receive text messages (SMS/MMS) including:

  • Appointment confirmations and reminders
  • Service-related notifications and follow-ups
  • AI-generated responses to your inquiries

Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies based on your interactions. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for assistance. Consent to receive messages is not a condition of purchasing any service. Your mobile phone number and opt-in data will not be shared with or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption:

  • Client and lead data is stored in encrypted databases (Supabase) with row-level security
  • All API communications use HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Payment data is processed by Stripe and never stored on our servers
  • Call recordings and transcripts are stored securely via Twilio
  • Access is restricted to authorized KalnyesGrowth team members only

Data Retention

We retain lead and inquiry data for up to 12 months from your last interaction with us. Client data is retained for the duration of our business relationship plus 12 months. Call recordings are retained for up to 90 days. You may request deletion at any time.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of any data we hold about you
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your data
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate data
  • Opt-out: Stop receiving messages from us at any time by replying "STOP" to any text message or emailing us

To exercise any of these rights, email kalnyesgrowth@gmail.com or call (540) 783-8835.

Third-Party Services

We use the following services to operate our business:

  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram): Social media platform APIs for reading and responding to comments and messages on our own business accounts
  • Twilio: Phone call handling, SMS/MMS messaging, and AI voice agent infrastructure
  • Stripe: Secure payment processing for subscriptions and one-time charges
  • Supabase: Encrypted database hosting for client and lead data

Each of these services maintains their own privacy policies and data protection standards.

Facebook and Instagram Data

Our use of Facebook and Instagram data is limited to:

  • Reading and responding to comments on our own business Page and Instagram account

  • Publishing content to our own accounts

We do not access data from any Facebook Pages or Instagram accounts other than our own. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond responding to your inquiry about our services.
