No contracts, ever. You can start a weekly plan and cancel or pause anytime with a simple text. We earn your business every time we show up.

We monitor the weather and reschedule automatically within the same week if conditions are unsafe. We'll always text you to let you know.

We accept Venmo, Zelle, cash, and all major credit/debit cards. You pay after each visit — no prepayment required.

Yes — we bring everything. Commercial-grade mowers, trimmers, blowers, and all supplies. You don't need to provide anything.

Absolutely. We serve HOA common areas, apartment complexes, and commercial properties throughout Stafford and Fredericksburg. Call for a custom quote.
