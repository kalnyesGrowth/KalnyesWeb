Our Story

Rooted in Community. Built on Reputation.

Ray's Siding Company has been a fixture in the Fredericksburg area for over 30 years. Owner Mike King personally visits every job site to assess your project — because after three decades in the business, he knows that precision measurements and understanding your vision from the start are what separate a good job from a great one.

Today, Ray's is a true family business. Mike's son Michael and his wife Chrissy are part of the team — bringing the same hands-on dedication that built the company's reputation across Spotsylvania, Fredericksburg, Stafford, Caroline, and King George counties.

From local sponsorships to community outreach, Ray's invests in the neighborhoods they serve. BBB Accredited since 1993 — before most of their competitors were in business.
