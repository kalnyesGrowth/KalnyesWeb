About JLS Builders

Justin Suggs — 15 Years of Fredericksburg Craftsmanship

Justin Suggs started JLS Builders because he believed homeowners in Fredericksburg deserved a contractor who actually answered the phone, showed up on time, and finished what he started. After 15 years, that reputation is still the foundation of every project we take on.
