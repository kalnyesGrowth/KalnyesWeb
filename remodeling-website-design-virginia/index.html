I already have a website. Why would I replace it?
Most remodeling contractor websites we've seen don't display the owner's license class, bury BBB ratings, show 2 of their 80 Google reviews, and break on mobile. All four of those issues cost you jobs every week. We rebuild from scratch to fix them — and you see the new site before paying.
Will my site show up on Google for remodeling searches?
Yes — the site is built with proper local SEO from day one: city-targeted page titles, meta descriptions, service-specific sections, Schema.org LocalBusiness markup, and location references throughout the content. These don't guarantee rank, but they're the foundation every ranking site is built on.
What if I do more than remodeling?
We cover whatever you do — roofing, siding, flooring, electrical, plumbing — each in its own section. Google indexes each service separately, which means more chances to appear in different searches.
How do you display my credentials?
Your license class (Class A, Class B), BBB accreditation and rating, certifications (CertainTeed, Trex Pro, NARI, etc.), years in business, and insurance status all appear as prominent badge-style elements in the hero section and credentials grid. Not in a footer. Not in an "About" paragraph. Front and center.
