About Wayne

20 Years in the Army. Now Protecting Your Vehicle.

Wayne Lewis served over two decades as a U.S. Army soldier — where precision, attention to detail, and getting it right the first time aren't optional. In 2020, he brought that same standard to Stafford as a certified mobile auto detailer.

Wayne holds IDA Certified Detailer (CD) and Skills Validated (SV) credentials — among the most respected certifications in the detailing industry worldwide. He's BBB A+ accredited and Detail King certified.

When Wayne details your vehicle, you're not getting a teenager with a pressure washer. You're getting a trained professional who takes your investment seriously.
