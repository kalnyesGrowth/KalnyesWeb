Our Story

Alex Hall — Certified Arborist, Stafford Native

Hall's Tree Service was built on one principle: treat every tree job like it's in your own backyard. Alex Hall has been climbing and caring for trees in Stafford and surrounding counties for over 15 years — and he earned his ISA Certified Arborist credential to make sure that expertise is backed by science, not just experience.

Every job is done by Alex and his trained crew — no subcontractors, no surprises. We carry full liability insurance and workers' comp so you're protected on every job.
