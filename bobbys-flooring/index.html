57 Years. One Family. One Name.

Bobby's Flooring & Carpet opened its doors in 1969 — the same year Neil Armstrong walked on the moon. More than five decades later, we're still the flooring company Fredericksburg families trust to get the job done right.

We're not a franchise. We're not a big-box retailer. We're your neighbors — and we've been taking care of this community's floors since before most of our competitors were born.

Every estimate is done in your home, measuring actual rooms, looking at actual subfloors. No surprises. No hidden costs. No salesmanship. Just honest advice from people who've been doing this for half a century.
