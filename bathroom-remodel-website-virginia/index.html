FAQ

Common Questions

Do I need professional photos of past bathroom projects to get started?

No. We can build the demo with a placeholder gallery so you see the layout, then you send us your best bathroom photos via text or email before launch. Phone photos are fine — we know what to do with them.

Can the site show the specific services I specialize in — like tile showers or freestanding tubs?

Yes — we build a dedicated service section with your specific specialties. Tile work, custom showers, vanity replacement, heated floors, soaking tubs — each gets its own card with a description that speaks to homeowners searching for that specific service.

Can homeowners request a quote directly from the site?

Yes — every site includes an estimate request form and/or Calendly booking link so homeowners can schedule a consultation directly from your site. You get the inquiry in your email immediately.

Does the $600 include my own domain name?

If you already have a domain, we deploy there. If not, we help you register one (about $15/year) and set everything up. The $600 covers the build and first year of hosting — your only ongoing cost after year one is $150/year for hosting.
