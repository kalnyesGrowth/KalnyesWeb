Our family
The Bourne family — four decades of HVAC integrity

Wayne Bourne started Comfort Heating & Cooling in 1980 with one principle: an HVAC company could operate with complete integrity and still be successful. Forty-four years later, that belief is proven.

Today Wayne's sons Craig and Matthew lead the residential and commercial divisions, bringing the same hands-on standard their father built. Eileen continues as President, keeping the family business accountable to the community it's served since the start.
