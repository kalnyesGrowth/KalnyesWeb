FAQ

Common Questions

What most homeowners ask before booking.

What types of jobs do you handle?
We handle most home repair and improvement jobs — drywall, painting, doors, electrical, plumbing, assembly, and more. If you're not sure, just call and describe the job. If it's outside our scope, we'll tell you upfront.
Are you licensed and insured?
Yes — fully licensed and insured in Virginia. We're also background checked. You can have complete peace of mind having us in your home.
How do you charge — hourly or by the job?
Most jobs are priced by the job so you know the cost upfront. For larger or more complex projects, we may charge hourly. Either way, we always give you a clear estimate before starting.
How fast can you come out?
Most non-emergency jobs are scheduled within the week. For urgent issues, call or text and we'll do our best to come out same day or next day.
Do you provide materials or do I buy them?
We can supply materials or work with materials you provide — whichever you prefer. We'll include material costs in your estimate if we're supplying them so there are no surprises.
