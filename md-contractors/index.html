About Us

Fredericksburg's trusted Class A contractor

MD Contractors Services LLC is a family-owned general contracting company based in Fredericksburg, VA. Owner Marcelo Hernandez built this business on one standard: do the job right, every time, with no shortcuts.

We hold a Class A contractor license and are fully insured and bonded — which means every job is backed by the highest credential Virginia offers to general contractors. We handle everything from quick repairs to full home remodels, all under one team and one point of contact.
