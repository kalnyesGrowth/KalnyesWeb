Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Who We Are

KalnyesGrowth ("we," "our," "us") is a web design agency based in Stafford, Virginia, operated by Jose Jessyt Morales Ricaldi. We build websites for local businesses and manage social media engagement through our Facebook and Instagram business accounts.

Contact: kalnyesgrowth@gmail.com | (540) 783-8835

What Data We Collect

When you interact with our social media content (Facebook Page, Instagram account), we may collect:

  • Public profile information: Your name and username as displayed on your social media profile
  • Comments: Comments you leave on our posts
  • Messages: Direct messages you send to or receive from our business accounts
  • Engagement data: Likes, shares, and other interactions with our content

We do not collect private information from your profile beyond what you make publicly visible.

How We Use Your Data

  • Responding to inquiries: When you comment on our posts or send us a message, we use your information to provide a personalized response about our services
  • Lead management: If you express interest in our services (e.g., commenting a keyword like "AUDIT" or "DEMO"), we log your inquiry to ensure timely follow-up
  • Service delivery: If you become a client, we use your contact information to deliver our web design services

We never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption:

  • Lead information is stored in Notion (encrypted at rest and in transit)
  • All API communications use HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Access is restricted to authorized KalnyesGrowth team members only

Data Retention

We retain lead and inquiry data for up to 12 months from your last interaction with us. Client data is retained for the duration of our business relationship plus 12 months. You may request deletion at any time.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of any data we hold about you
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your data
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate data
  • Opt-out: Stop receiving messages from us at any time by replying "STOP"

To exercise any of these rights, email kalnyesgrowth@gmail.com or call (540) 783-8835.

Third-Party Services

We use the following services to operate our business:

  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram): Social media platform APIs for reading and responding to comments and messages on our own business accounts
  • Notion: Secure database for managing leads and content
  • Vercel: Cloud hosting for our automation tools
  • Anthropic (Claude): AI assistance for drafting personalized responses

Each of these services maintains their own privacy policies and data protection standards.

Facebook & Instagram Data

Our use of Facebook and Instagram data is limited to:

  • Reading and responding to comments on our own business Page and Instagram account
  • Sending direct messages to users who interact with our content
  • Publishing content to our own accounts

We do not access data from any Facebook Pages or Instagram accounts other than our own. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond responding to your inquiry about our services.

Data Deletion Instructions

To request deletion of your data:

  • Email kalnyesgrowth@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request"
  • Include your name and the platform (Facebook or Instagram) you interacted with us on
  • We will confirm deletion within 5 business days
