Our method
Why soft wash — not just pressure

Most pressure washing companies blast everything with high pressure. That works on driveways — but it damages siding, strips paint, breaks seals on windows, and can force water behind your walls.

Red Door Pro Wash uses a soft wash method for delicate surfaces: low-pressure water combined with biodegradable cleaning solutions that kill mold, mildew, and algae at the root — not just on the surface.

The result lasts longer. The surface stays protected. And we back it with a 12-month warranty — something high-pressure companies can't offer because they know the problem comes back faster.

🚫 What high-pressure washing damages:

Vinyl siding · Wood siding · Roof shingles · Window seals · Painted surfaces · Masonry mortar · Composite decking

✅ What soft wash protects and cleans:

All of the above — safely, effectively, and with results that last. Jonathan's military background means following the right process every time, not taking shortcuts.
