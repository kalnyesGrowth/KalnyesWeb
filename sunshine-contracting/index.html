30 Years of Building Better Homes in Woodbridge

The family started Sunshine Contracting in 1993 with a commitment to doing every job the right way — no shortcuts, no substandard materials, no broken promises. Three decades later, our BBB A+ rating reflects what happens when a contractor actually lives up to that commitment.

We work throughout Woodbridge, Manassas, Stafford, and surrounding Northern Virginia communities. Most of our work comes from repeat customers and referrals — which is exactly what happens when you build trust over three decades instead of just chasing the next job.

From a single bathroom remodel to a full home addition — every Sunshine project gets the same family treatment.
