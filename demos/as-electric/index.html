What We Do

Residential & Commercial Electrical Services

From basic repairs to full installations, we handle every electrical job with precision, safety, and code compliance.

Electrical Panel Upgrades

Upgrade outdated panels to handle modern electrical demands safely. We install 100-400 amp panels with full code compliance and inspection.

Lighting Installation

Interior and exterior lighting design, accent lighting, landscape lighting, and LED retrofits for homes and commercial spaces.

EV Charger Installation

Level 2 EV charging station installation for your home or business. Proper circuit sizing, dedicated lines, and permit handling included.

Residential Wiring

Complete home wiring for new construction, remodels, and additions. Outlets, switches, ceiling fans, and dedicated circuits.

Commercial Electrical

Electrical solutions for offices, restaurants, retail stores, and warehouses. Tenant improvements, signage, and power distribution.

Backup Generators

Whole-home and commercial generator installation and maintenance. Automatic transfer switches for seamless power during outages.

Troubleshooting & Repair

Expert diagnosis of electrical issues including flickering lights, tripped breakers, faulty wiring, and power surges.

Outlets & Switches

GFCI outlets, USB outlets, smart switches, dimmers, and 220V receptacles. Proper installation with ground fault protection.

Audio/Visual Installation

Structured wiring for home theaters, smart home systems, security cameras, and connected home technology.
