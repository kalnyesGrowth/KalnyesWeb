FAQ
Common Questions
Do I need to move my furniture?
We move all furniture away from the walls and protect everything with drop cloths. You don't need to move anything before we arrive — we handle it.
How long does a whole-home interior take?
A typical 2,000–2,500 sq ft home takes 3–4 days for a full interior. We work efficiently and don't rush the prep — that's what makes the finish last.
What paint brands do you use?
We use Sherwin-Williams, Benjamin Moore, and Behr Premium on all projects. We buy the best quality because cheap paint means more coats, faster fading, and callbacks — we'd rather do it right once.
How do I pay?
50% deposit to schedule, 50% when the job is complete and you've done the walkthrough. We accept check, Venmo, Zelle, and all major credit cards.
Do you provide the paint or do I buy it?
We provide all paint and materials — it's included in your estimate. You tell us the colors (or let us help you pick), and we handle everything from there.
