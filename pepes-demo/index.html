FAQ
Common Questions
How far in advance should I book?
Weekends — especially Saturdays in summer — book 2–4 weeks out. For last-minute requests, always call us directly. We'll do our best to accommodate.
What if it rains on my event day?
We monitor the forecast closely. If there's lightning or heavy rain in the forecast, we'll work with you to reschedule at no charge. Light drizzle usually isn't a problem for most units.
Do I need to provide anything?
For water slides, you'll need access to a standard garden hose. For all units, we need a flat area of grass or concrete. We handle everything else.
Is there a deposit required?
Yes — 50% deposit to reserve your date, with the remaining balance due on delivery day. We accept Venmo, Zelle, cash, and all major cards.
Do you serve HOA communities?
Yes! We carry full liability insurance and can provide a Certificate of Insurance for your HOA on request. Just let us know when you book.
